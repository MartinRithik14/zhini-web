Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview

ZHINI ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by atom8 IT solutions, is dedicated to protecting household privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how user information is collected, stored, encrypted, and utilized across our mobile applications, web portals, and associated services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, phone number, email address, and general residential area or apartment society.
  • Appliance & Document Data: Appliance brand, model numbers, serial numbers, uploaded purchase invoices, warranty cards, and images captured for AI document parsing.
  • Service Records: Repair requests, scheduled maintenance timestamps, and digital service logs.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: App crash logs, operating system versions, and performance analytics to ensure application stability.

3. Camera & Storage Permissions

ZHINI requests device camera and storage permissions solely to enable users to photograph or upload appliance nameplates, invoices, and warranty documents for OCR parsing and secure cloud storage. We do not access, scan, or retrieve private media outside of explicitly uploaded assets.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To extract warranty durations, model numbers, and purchase dates using hybrid AI models.
  • To alert households before warranty expiration or recommend routine preventive upkeep.
  • To connect users with authorized brand service centres or verified repair partners.
  • To safeguard verifiable maintenance histories for domestic appliances.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. When a service request is placed, only necessary details (appliance type, fault description, and contact location) are shared with the chosen service center or technician.

6. Security & Retention

Uploaded invoices and documents are securely stored using enterprise-grade cloud storage with industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption in transit and AES encryption at rest. Users retain full control over their data and may request deletion of their account and documents at any time.
