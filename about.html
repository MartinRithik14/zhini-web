OUR ORIGIN & PURPOSE

A Single Digital Relationship Around Every Machine in the Modern Home.

We rely on dozens of machines daily to keep life running smoothly. Yet when an appliance breaks, everything stops. Families are left navigating a disconnected maze of missing paper bills, expired warranties, and unverified contacts. We built ZHINI to put an end to that friction.

THE REALITY OF URBAN LIVING

Urban homes own 10 to 25+ appliances. Ownership shouldn’t feel like paperwork.

From air conditioners and refrigerators to RO purifiers and washing machines, modern households run on complex appliances. Yet, the support system around them hasn't evolved in decades.

Invoices end up misplaced in drawers. Warranty dates quietly lapse unnoticed. And when a critical machine breaks, families face stressful friction: paying high unexpected rates without transparency, or trusting an unverified contact with zero service history.

ZHINI turns this chaos into complete peace of mind by giving every appliance a living digital profile with proactive alerts, smart document storage, and verified service support.
